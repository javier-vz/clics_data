# Paper plan — empirical row occupancy in colexification networks

**Working title:** Conditional row-occupancy statistics in cross-linguistic colexification networks

**Core question:** How does the occupancy distribution of form–concept associations differ from a random surjective allocation after controlling exactly the observed association count and number of active forms?

## 1. Introduction

- Zipfian reuse/ambiguity trade-off as context.
- Prior statistical-mechanical row-occupancy model as a theoretical motivation, not evidence of equilibrium in actual languages.
- Empirical test using CLICS: marginal occupancy vs connectivity in concept projections.

## 2. Materials and methods

- Exact source version, normalization and filtering, A/V/M definitions.
- Conditional-surjection occupancy null: independent exact simulation.
- Degree-preserving bipartite edge-swap null: finite-length MCMC, sensitivity analyses.
- Observables, replication counts, Monte Carlo p-values, multiple-testing and dependency caveats.

## 3. Results (fill ONLY after the complete run)

- Coverage and occupancy descriptive distributions.
- Observed concentration vs conditional null and per-language ratios.
- Abundance of multi-concept forms and maximum form occupancy.
- Component sizes and isolated concepts vs degree-preserving null.
- Threshold robustness and family/macroarea exploratory distributions.

## 4. Discussion

- Interpret magnitude and heterogeneity of observed/null deviations; revisit the result in light of coverage, phonetic normalization, family dependence.
- Distinguish form reuse distributions from topology conditional on degree sequences.
- Connect cautiously to theoretical row-occupancy results; do not conflate observed lexical statistics with equilibrium phase transitions.

## 5. Conclusion

- Report actual measured regularities and limitations without predetermined anti-random claims.
